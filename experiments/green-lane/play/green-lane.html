PORT REGULATIONS

Twelve crossings. Each player runs the same cargo — the 2, 3, 4, 5 (legal goods, worth 1/2/3/4) and the 7 and A (contraband, worth 6 and 13) — one crate per crossing, face down. You ship on odd crossings; they ship on even ones. The other side is customs, holding two inspection tokens for the whole night.

Waved through: the crate slides into the shipper's warehouse unopened. Nobody learns what it was. It banks its value.
Opened — legal goods: false alarm. The crate is impounded (it scores nobody) and customs pays the shipper the 5-point fine.
Opened — contraband: seized. The crate's value scores for customs, and the shipper pays the 5-point fine.

Out of tokens, customs waves everything; down to one crate, it ships itself under seal. Final score is your banked value + what you seized + net fines, minus the same for them. Highest total takes the night.

Both starting hands are public. All the hiding is in the choosing.
