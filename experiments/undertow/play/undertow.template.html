SAILING ORDERS (3½ rules)

Full deck, four hands of thirteen. Whoever holds the 2♣ leads first.

1. Follow the led suit if you can. Highest trump — else highest card of the led suit — takes the trick and leads next.
2. The undertow: when a trick is gathered, its lowest card (earliest played breaks ties) names the trump suit for the next trick.
3. Most tricks wins the voyage.
½. The first trick is played with no trump — the tide isn't in yet.

Aces high, twos low. Every 2 is a rudder: high cards buy the present, low cards steer the future. Can't win a trick? Your throwaway can still drag the tide to a suit you own.
