# Higher-order knowledge

**Tier 4 — low impact, defer until forced.**

"Does P know that Q knows X?" is real in epistemic logic and
relevant to some game-theoretic analysis, but not needed by any
rule in the standard-deck corpus. Add when a counterexample
surfaces.

**Coup (bluff game, still didn't force it).** Coup is a bluffing game,
so it was a natural suspect. But second-order reasoning in Coup —
"does my opponent believe I hold the Duke?" — is *strategy* over the
information sets, not an input to any rule's legality or resolution. No
Coup rule reads what another player knows. The question still awaits a
rule that genuinely consults "P knows that Q knows X" (Hanabi's hint
conventions, Sheepshead's called-partner knowledge).
