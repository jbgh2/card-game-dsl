term: Honest Play
definition: The engine's standing assumption about players: a seat discloses what the rules say it must, and information reaches it through its zones' declared [[projection]]s and the [[observation-event]]s moves emit, and through nothing else. What a seat derives from the rules, the running [[phase]], the turn and its own observations is the game, not a breach. A rule that gates, counts or routes on another seat's concealed cards is therefore a mis-modelled rule rather than an information leak — a rulebook makes the holder announce or show, and the rule reads the announcement. Relaxing the assumption (signalling, concealment, peeking) is out of scope.
