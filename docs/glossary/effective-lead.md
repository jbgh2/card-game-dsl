term: Effective Lead
definition: The arrival that SET a trick's class — the first that is a trump or carries a [[follow-class]]. Not the first card played: a class-less card leads to nothing and the next card sets the class instead. Distinct from a game's `led_suit` state variable, which is the literal first card's printed suit; where the two differ, the Effective Lead is what follow legality and the winner both read.
