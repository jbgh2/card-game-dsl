term: Simultaneous Selection
definition: Each seat's chosen [[transfer]] inside `each <role> simultaneously:` — committed before any other seat's pick is shown, and effected as one act (decisions.md "Simultaneous moves and atomic effect"). Spelled `simultaneous` in the `asked` [[observation-event]], and read aloud as a commit rather than a move, because what a seat does here is commit blind. Its destination is settled only once every seat has chosen, which the event carries as no zone at all. Not "simultaneous pass" — `pass` is the bidding and climbing move.
