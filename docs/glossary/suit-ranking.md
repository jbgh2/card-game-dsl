term: Suit Ranking
definition: A game's declared, context-free order of its suits, strongest first — the `suit ranking:` clause, read only by `suit_value(s)`, which numbers the weakest listed suit 0. It lists every suit the deck declares and may leave out the deck's special suits. It is never the order of the `Suit` move-parameter domain or of `for each suit`, which keep deck order. The suit-side sibling of the `ranking:` clause; never bare "suit order", since `order` is reserved.
