term: Library Origin
definition: What `resolve` records on each piece of library text it splices into a game: the library, the definition holding the text in the designer's words, the game's `uses` line, and the zones the library's contract names. It goes on a family library's move types, functions, procedures and provided state declarations, and on every library rule a game activates, the standard library's included. It is the second fact allowed across the splice, beside the [[definition-contract]] stamp. Resolve and the type layer read it so that a verdict reading a game clause or declaration reaches the author who owns the fact. A seat or team number, or a rank or suit written as a string, goes to the library's author. A card field the game's pieces lack goes to the game's `uses` line.
