term: Operator's Milestone
definition: The one open milestone the operator may hold beside the direction review's two — opened by the operator alone, for a unit the review did not promote: a tiered issue the front never reaches, work important and not urgent, or a unit the operator wants done. The shape of any [[active-epic]] (one epic and its parts, a Destination or `Upkeep` line, a finish line), named `Operator's Milestone` in its description's first line, taken by the fleet like any ACTIVE row, and closed by the usual rule (the taker on a checkable finish line, the review on a judgment one) or by the operator at any time; outside the one-Upkeep bound and outside #143. The direction review reads it every verdict as an instrument — what it held, how it closed, whether that says the queue is ordered wrong — and promotes a class that lands there twice. Distinct from an [[active-epic]] the review opened from the head of #143.
