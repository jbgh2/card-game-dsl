**Why the word survives its own prohibition.** F-19 retired "option" as a loose
spelling of [[candidate]], and that retirement holds everywhere. What it never
reached is poker's rule of the same name, which is a fact about three corpus
games rather than a way of saying "legal move" — so the word is scoped rather
than retired outright. The article separates the senses in reading: *the* option
is the rule, and a bare "option" is the retired spelling.

The rule it names is stated in `docs/decisions.md`, "Named functions": a forced
post placed before the round begins is not a turn taken within it, so the posting
seat stays pending and keeps the right to raise its own post. Pagat grants it to
the big blind in as many words; it is silent on the bring-in, so
`docs/games/seven-card-stud.cardlang` pins that half as the variant's own rule.
