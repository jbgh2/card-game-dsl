definition: The dated record of one operator ruling, under `docs/adr/` — numbered, immutable once landed except its Status line, carrying the ruling in the operator's words where given, the alternatives that lost and the measurement that killed each, the consequences, and the issue and PR that hold the act and the counsel. Every [[merge-lane]] A ruling has one. Not a plan, a verdict, a lesson or state. Abbreviated ADR.
