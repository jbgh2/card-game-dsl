term: Kernel Definition Contract
definition: A signature the kernel states and only the game answers, by writing `function <name>` with that signature — a [[definition-contract]] with the kernel as the party requiring. The game answers by the name alone; the answer is typed against the contract's row and held hermetic, a fact of the card and public state. The rows are `kernel_contracts.KERNEL_DEFINITION_CONTRACTS`: the three facts of a [[trick-order]] and the game's [[card-points]]. The `card_points { }` table is the one block spelling, and it lowers to the answering function. Never a Builtin's: the two registries are pinned disjoint, and a family library may define none, writing the row in its `requires` instead.
