term: Headnote
definition: The plain-words compression that closes every persona counsel — after the law reporter's headnote, written after the judgment and from it, at the head of the report, no part of the law. Written last, never before; closes the reply at the table, stands at the head of a record; where headnote and counsel disagree, the counsel governs. Carries the facts the seat's charter says must survive and the bottom line — the verdict, the strongest case against it and its cost, and the operator's decision — with the measured numbers and their denominators, no citations. Contrast the reserved word [[brief]], the instruction handed down to a worker.
