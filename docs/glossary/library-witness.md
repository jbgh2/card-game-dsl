---
term: Library Witness
definition: A corpus or family game that `uses` a family library, read only by the attribution step. One that checks clean proves sound every library definition its own text reaches, so a refusal of such a definition in another game, with no crossing to tell it at, is that game's, told on its `uses` line. A library none of whose witnesses checks clean is unproven: its refusals stay in its file, with a warning on the `uses` line. Found by glob, never declared.
