term: Board Family
definition: A row of the closed `BOARDS` registry that a `board:` clause names (`grid(8, 8)`, `morris9`, `hex_rhombus(11)`): its argument arity and bounds, and the builder of the board entry, whose cells mint `cell` and whose declared parts -- line lengths, adjacency, movement frame, each seat's borders -- decide which board verbs the family answers. A verb reading a part the family does not declare is refused at the call, naming the family and the verbs it answers.
