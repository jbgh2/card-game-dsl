term: Round
definition: The kernel card-play loop: the `round` keyword and its two **forms** — **trick form**, **climb form**. A bidding, betting or polling ring is no form of `round`; it is `turns` with an `offer` body. "Round" never means "a round of the game"; that concept is a [[hand]]. The forms are distinct AST nodes — `TrickRound` / `ClimbRound`, each carrying only its own form's clauses. Surface keyword unchanged; a surface word for trick stays parked on the second family.
