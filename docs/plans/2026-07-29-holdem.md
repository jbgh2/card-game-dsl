# Texas Hold'em — the corpus's next game

Issue #143 item 1 ("pick the next game for its unblocks"), Hold'em branch.
Operator chose Hold'em over Euchre and Spider on 2026-07-29.

## Why this game

Three pressures, none of which the corpus carries today:

1. **A zone owned by nobody, read in every player's hand evaluation.** The
   community board is the first five-card instance; Leduc's single face-up
   card is the only precedent.
2. **`poker_betting`'s first differently-shaped consumer.** Kuhn, Leduc and
   Stud all open a street with no standing bet. Hold'em opens pre-flop with
   the blinds already posted, and its action order is positional (left of the
   big blind pre-flop, left of the dealer after) rather than card-derived.
   An abstraction with three same-shaped consumers has never been tested.
3. **The second side-pot instance.** Stud is the first; a second is what
   corpus-first promotion of the shared poker math waits on.

## Scope walls — stated up front

- **`open-questions/move-level-visibility.md` is NOT resolved here.** The
  "show one, show all" showdown rule becomes *writable* against Hold'em's
  showdown, and the question file records where it would bind. It does not
  record a resolution: the candidates file already argues the rule is
  exercisable in Stud alone, so landing Hold'em is not by itself the answer.
- **No new library zone type.** The board is `Discard` (identity to all,
  unowned), which is exactly what Leduc already does
  (`board : Discard // the face-up community card`). A `Community` type with
  `capacity: 5` was considered and rejected: it would buy one overfill wall
  and cost a second spelling for one concept inside a single family. The
  capacity residual is recorded in the completeness ledger, not built.
- **Chips stay plain `Integer` state** (the Stud/Leduc convention), not a
  resource-zone subsystem.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **Runs.** Random playouts terminate over a seed sweep; total chips are
   invariant every hand (the falsifiable invariant, per `stud.py`).
2. **Regression-clean.** Bare `mypy` and full `pytest -q` green. The poker-math
   extraction is a pure refactor, so **Stud's `seven-card-stud_hands.json` and
   `seven-card-stud.ir.json` stay byte-identical**.
3. **Info sets derive.** `tests/openspiel_ready/test_holdem.py` proves it over
   the shared harness. Hole cards are `Hand` (identity to owner, count to
   others); the board is `Discard` (identity to all), so it carries no hidden
   state; the showdown reveal is observable as a movement event.

## Rules source

Pagat, <https://www.pagat.com/poker/variants/texasholdem.html>, fetched
2026-07-29. Facts taken from it, not from memory:

- Blinds: small blind left of dealer, big blind next. **Heads-up reverses it**
  — the dealer posts the small blind.
- Action: pre-flop begins left of the big blind; every later street begins with
  the first active player left of the dealer.
- Deal: burn, two hole cards each; burn, flop (3); burn, turn; burn, river.
- Fixed limit: one bet plus three raises per street; limits double from the
  turn onward.
- Showdown: best five from the seven available (both hole cards, one, or none).

`raise_cap` in `poker_betting` counts **aggressions including the opening
bet** — `bet` sets `raises := 1`, `raise` gates on `raises < raise_cap`
(Kuhn 1, Leduc 2, Stud 3). "One bet plus three raises" is therefore
`raise_cap : 4`, and pre-flop the big blind takes the `raises := 1` slot
exactly as Stud's bring-in does.

## Shape

3 players, 100 chips each, blinds 2/5, limits 5/5/10/10, `raise_cap` 4.
Multi-hand to a single winner (the Stud model, not Leduc's single-hand
`net`), because that is what produces the all-ins that side pots need — and it
is what makes the heads-up blind reversal reachable, once a player busts.

## What the glob-derived pins demanded (found by running them, not by planning)

Adding a corpus game reddens more closed-domain registries than the task list
anticipated. Each fired loudly and by construction — which is the machinery
working, and worth recording as the list a future game addition should expect:

- `tests/test_ranking_conventions.py::_PRE_MIGRATION` — a frozen
  (convention, deck, expansion) row per convention-declaring corpus game.
- `tests/test_primitive_narrowing.py` — `NARROWED` (per implementation site) and
  `MIGRATED` (per primitive). Both are `xfail(strict=True)`-driven, so a module
  written narrowed from birth XPASSes and FAILS until it is declared narrowed.
- `tests/openspiel_ready/test_coverage.py` — one proof module per registered
  game, both directions.
- `tests/test_signatures.py` + `tests/test_piece_content_guards.py` — the
  name/signature/flavor-partition reconciliation. **Verified red first**: with
  the two names in `STDLIB_CALL_FUNCS` alone, four assertions across the two
  modules failed and named both members.

The flavor wall's misuse probe needed no new test: it is derived per member
(`parametrize(sorted(DECK_ONLY_CALL_FUNCS))` in
`test_deck_only_call_rejected_in_piece_game`), so `holdem_pot_share` is probed
against a piece-flavored game the moment it is classified.

## What review caught that the plan's oracles did not

Two P1 rule defects, both found by Codex on PR #195, both confirmed by
measurement before being fixed. Each is worth recording because of the shape
they share: **a broken game still conserved chips, still terminated, and still
looked legal.** The plan's headline oracle could not have found either.

1. **The button paused on dead seats.** Rotating the persistent `dealer` through
   every physical seat and mapping dead seats forward is not the same as
   stepping it along the live ring: with seats 1 and 2 live, positions 0,1,2 map
   to buttons 1,2,1, so seat 1 took the button — and heads-up the small blind —
   on two hands of every three. Measured: the heads-up button sequence was
   `[1,2,1,1,2,1,…]`.
2. **A lone live player who still owed the blind was skipped.** The pre-flop
   guard was `can_act >= 2`, but the blinds are the one forced post that leaves
   a standing bet, so a big blind all-in for its whole post leaves ONE live
   player who still owes and must be allowed to call or fold. Measured: 17 real
   decisions across 12 seeds were being skipped. The guard is now the round's
   own `until` terminator negated, which is the general form — every later
   street opens with nobody owing, so there the two readings agree.

A third defect surfaced in the FIX, not the code: the first
`test_button_alternates_strictly_heads_up` deduplicated consecutive identical
rows, which collapsed exactly the repeats it existed to catch — it passed under
the reddening plant. It now segments on the engine's own `hand_end` counter.
This is the case for running a red-under rather than asserting one: the test was
plausible, targeted, and vacuous.

## Recorded residuals

- **Community-board capacity is unbounded.** `Discard` is unbounded, so a
  sixth board card would be a silent overfill rather than a loud one. Not
  built (see the scope wall). Note in passing: `_deposit`'s overfill message
  hardcodes the hint `guard the move ('<zone> is empty')`, which is only right
  for a capacity-1 zone — latent, unreachable while `Cell` is the sole bounded
  row, and deliberately not fixed here.
- **`move-level-visibility` stays open** (see the scope wall).
